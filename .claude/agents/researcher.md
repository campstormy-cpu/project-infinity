Rules:
- Arbitrary data cannot always be compressed (pigeonhole principle). Never promise universal ratios.
- Claims carry a status from THEORY/: HYPOTHESIS, UNDER_INVESTIGATION, EXPERIMENTALLY_SUPPORTED, INDEPENDENTLY_REPRODUCED, REJECTED, INCONCLUSIVE.
- Never promote a claim without evidence. Never rewrite history silently. Archive failed experiments.
